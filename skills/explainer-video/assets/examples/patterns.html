The records behind a loan

Four records work together each time a member borrows a book.

The short version

  • A library owns many books.
  • A member borrows a book by creating a loan.A loan links one member to one copy of a book.
  • The loan ends when the book comes back.

Four records

Each one has its own colour for the rest of this video.

Library

A branch that owns books.

Book

One physical copy on a shelf.

Member

A person with a library card.

Loan

Who has which book, and until when.

How they link

A book stores its library's ID, so each copy belongs to exactly one library.

A 21-day loan on the calendar

The loan starts on the day the member borrows the book.

It is due 21 days later, counting the first day.

A renewal adds another 21 days from the old due date.
