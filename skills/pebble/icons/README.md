Ten original single-colour icons on a 24px grid: 1.75px rounded strokes, no fills, `stroke="currentColor"`. They take the colour of the text around them, so inline them as `<svg>`; an `<img>` renders them black. Set: home, search, bell, user, calendar, chart, sliders, plus, check, trash. Show them at 18px in buttons and menus, 20px elsewhere, 14px in chips, or inside a 40px `pb-icontile`. New icons follow the same grid and stroke, with no fill.
